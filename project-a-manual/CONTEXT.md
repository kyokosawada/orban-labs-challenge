# Notes

A personal note-taking store. Notes are written, edited, labelled, and found again by
searching their text or narrowing to a label.

## Language

**Note**:
A single piece of written content belonging to the person who wrote it, carrying a title and
a body.
_Avoid_: Entry, document, post, item

**Tag**:
A short label attached to a Note to group it with others. A Note may carry several. A Tag
exists in its own right rather than as a property of one Note, so it outlives the Notes that
used it and may currently be attached to none. A Tag is identified by its normalised form -
trimmed and lowercased - so `Work` and `work` are the same Tag and the spelling as typed is
not retained.
_Avoid_: Label, category, keyword, topic

**Tag in use**:
A Tag currently attached to at least one Note that has not been deleted. Only these are
offered as filters, because a Tag attached to nothing would be a filter that returns nothing.

**Deleted Note**:
A Note the writer has removed. It is retained in storage so an accidental removal stays
recoverable by hand, but it is gone as far as the product is concerned: it appears in no
listing, matches no search, and cannot be fetched or restored.
_Avoid_: Archived, trashed, hidden
